# Healthtech Knowledge Base — User Guide

A simple guide for healthcare staff using the Knowledge Base and chatbot assistant. No technical background needed.

 1. Signing In

 1. Open the Knowledge Base link given to you by your facility.
 1. If you already have an account, enter your **email** and **password** and click **Sign In**.
 1. If you don't have an account yet, click **"Don't have an account? Create one"**, fill in your name, email, and a password, choose **Viewer** (to read articles and use the chatbot) or **Editor** (if you'll also write articles), and click **Create Account**.

 1. Finding an Answer — Searching Articles

 1. On the Knowledge Base page, type what you're looking for in the search bar (e.g. "vitals" or "patient registration").
 1. As you type, a dropdown will suggest matching articles — click one to open it directly.
 1. You can also narrow results using the **Category**, **Content Type**, and **Tag** dropdowns above the article list.
 1. Click any article title to read the full guide.

**Tip:** If your search doesn't find anything, try simpler or different words, or ask the chat assistant instead — it can often help even when search comes up empty.

 1. Using the Chat Assistant

 1. Click the round chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of any page.
 1. Type your question in plain language (e.g. *"How do I capture patient vitals?"*) and press **Send**.
 1. The assistant will answer using only approved articles — if it doesn't know something, it will say so honestly rather than guess.
 1. Below each answer, you may see linked article titles — click one to read the full guide it came from.
 1. Use the **👍 / 👎** buttons to tell us if the answer was helpful.
 1. You can ask follow-up questions in the same conversation — the assistant remembers what you've already discussed.
 1. Click the small copy icon in the chat header to copy the full conversation — useful if you need to share it with support.

 1. Rating an Article

At the bottom of any article, click the star that matches how helpful it was (1 to 5 stars), optionally add a comment, and click **Submit Feedback**.

 1. Downloading an Article as PDF

Open any article and click the **Download PDF** button near the title to save or print it for offline use.

 1. For Editors — Creating and Editing Articles

## Requires an Editor or Admin account

1. Click **Manage Articles** in the top navigation.
2. Click **+ New Article**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — the slug (URL) fills in automatically
   - **Content** — write in Markdown (plain text with simple formatting like `Heading` or `**bold**`)
   - **Category** — choose an existing one, or type a new name and click **+ Add Category**
   - **Tags** — click existing tags to select them, or type a new one and click **+ Add Tag**
   - **Content Type** — choose How-To, SOP, FAQ, Troubleshooting, Feature Reference, or Release Notes
   - **Product Version** *(optional)* — which system version this applies to
4. To add an image, click **Upload Image**, select a file, and it will be inserted automatically into your content.
5. Set **Status** to **Draft** to save your work without publishing, or **Published** to submit it.

**Important:** As an Editor, if you choose "Published," your article is automatically sent for **Admin review** first — it will show as "Pending Review" until an Admin approves it. You'll see it move to "Published" once approved, or back to "Draft" with a reason if changes are needed.

 1. For Admins — Reviewing and Publishing

 1. In **Manage Articles**, click **"Show Pending Review only"** to see articles waiting for your approval.
 1. Click **Approve** to publish an article, or **Reject** to send it back to the author with an optional reason.
 1. You can also **Archive** any published article to remove it from public view without deleting its history — archived articles can be restored by an admin if needed.
 1. If you edit an article and want to undo your last change, click **↺ Revert to previous version** in the content editor.

 1. For Admins — Managing Users

 1. Click **Manage Users** in the navigation.
 1. Use the role dropdown next to any user to change them between **Viewer**, **Editor**, and **Admin**.
 1. Click **Deactivate** to temporarily block a user from logging in, or **Reactivate** to restore access.

 1. For Admins — Analytics

Click **Analytics** to see:

- Your most-viewed articles
- Articles with low ratings that may need improvement
- What people are searching for most
- Searches that returned no results (a sign you may need new content)
- Articles that haven't been updated in over 180 days
- Chatbot questions the assistant couldn't answer — a good source of ideas for new articles

 Need More Help?

If you're stuck on something not covered here, ask the chat assistant — or contact your facility's IT administrator.
